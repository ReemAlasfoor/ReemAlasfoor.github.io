About

A mathematician who builds with data

I'm completing a BSc in Mathematics at the University of Bahrain, with a foundation spanning linear algebra, real analysis, and graph theory. Alongside my degree, I recently finished the General Assembly Data Science Bootcamp, where I built out the practical side of that foundation — Python, statistical modeling, and machine learning applied to real problems.

What sets my approach apart is a background in 3D spatial visualization: I use Blender to animate vectors and abstract structures for my education channel, AlasfoorMATH, which means I don't just compute a result — I can show what it means geometrically. Outside of work, I explore the mathematics behind mechanical puzzles like the Rubik's Cube, track the night sky as an amateur astronomer, and I'm currently learning Chinese and German.
